# Domain glossary (so icons do not collide)

Archaser has **two products** on one account (sometimes only one is on):

1. **Collection** — collect unpaid invoices from customers.
2. **Credit insurance** — the seller is insured against buyer default; track policies, limits, reporting, claims.

A **credit-only** account hides collection dashboards (financial, operations, control center, disputes, legal, PTP).

## Objects

| Term | What it is | Icon must not look like |
|---|---|---|
| **Customer** | A company that bought on credit (the buyer). Has invoices, contacts, sometimes a policy. | Social user / team avatar |
| **Invoice** | A bill. Open amount is what is still owed. | Retail till receipt |
| **Payment** | Money received against invoices | Crypto coin |
| **Collection period / phase** | Where the account sits in collection (automated, agent, PTP, dispute, legal) | Game level / funnel clipart |
| **Activity sequence** | Automated emails/SMS/steps to collect | Robot / magic wand |
| **Agent** | Internal person working the account | Customer / buyer |
| **Dispute** | The **customer** says the invoice is wrong (amount, goods, etc.). Internal + portal. | Court gavel (that reads as Legal) |
| **Promise to pay (PTP)** | Customer promised to pay by a date | Handshake “deal closed” |
| **Legal** | Collection **escalated to legal** (lawyers / cases), not insurance | Same gavel as Disputes |
| **Insurance policy** | Contract with the insurer: limits, insured %, terms, fees | Health insurance / umbrella cliché unless distinctive |
| **Limit / capacity** | How much you may sell on cover vs how much AR sits on the policy | Battery / fuel gauge |
| **Top-up** | Extra temporary limit on top of the primary policy | Plus badge only if it stays unique |
| **At risk / terms / reporting** | Credit KPIs: exposure vs rules (overdue, max payment term, reporting to insurer) | Skull / siren |
| **Claim** | The **seller files with the insurer** after a loss (usually an overdue invoice). Not a customer dispute. | Medical claim, shopping complaint |
| **Control Center** | Broken master data (no contact, invoice without customer, bounced email) | Hospital heart monitor unless you replace it with a “data health / issues” mark |
| **Portfolio Health** | Analytics over a date range: health, no coverage, utilization, cost of cover | Same as Credit Dashboard |
| **Report builder** | User-built tabular reports on these objects | Pie chart on every “report” icon |

## Easy mix-ups (never merge these three)

- **Dispute** = customer vs seller about an invoice  
- **Legal** = seller vs customer in legal collection  
- **Claim** = seller vs **insurer** on a policy  

Three different silhouettes.

## Metaphor hints (starting point — challenge these)

- Customer: building or named-entity mark, not a head  
- Invoice: document with a single amount line, not a grocery receipt  
- Credit / policy: shield or stamped document — **not `$`**  
- Claim: document + shield, or document leaving toward an insurer — not a gavel  
- Dispute: invoice with a conflict mark (two marks / pause), not a courtroom  
- Legal: scales or brief, distinct from dispute  
- PTP: calendar with a check, not a handshake  
- Overdue: invoice + time, not a red alarm  
- Limit/capacity: bracket or ceiling over a stack, not a battery
