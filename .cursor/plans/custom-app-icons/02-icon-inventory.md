## Customer portal

Home, Invoices, Payment, Promise to Pay, Disputes, Submit a Dispute, Wrong Contact, Language. Calmer than the internal app; still the same family.

## KPI / card metaphors (priority 2)

Cards sit on colored **icon tiles**. Draw simple marks for:

- Open receivables / total due
- Overdue amount / overdue invoices / overdue accounts
- Due today / this week / this month
- Promise to pay
- Open disputes
- Collection activity success / undelivered messages
- Credit: at risk, compliant, capacity / limit, terms, no policy, reporting, limit warnings, health index

Reuse one **invoice**, **customer**, **overdue**, **policy/limit** glyph across cards instead of a unique doodle per KPI.

## Do not custom-theme (unless asked)

Search, filter, clear, add, edit, delete, close, checkbox, chevron, export, help. Universals should stay familiar.
